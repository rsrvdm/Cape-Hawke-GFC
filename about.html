Our story

Built by anglers, for anglers.

Cape Hawke GFC was established in 2026 by a group of local anglers who wanted a welcoming, family-friendly home for game fishing on the Mid Coast.

Our history

Established 2026

Cape Hawke Game Fishing Club was established in 2026 by a passionate group of local anglers who shared a common vision: to create a welcoming, family-friendly club that promotes game fishing, supports local businesses and encourages participation in our sport for generations to come.

Although our club is new, our ambition is significant. We are committed to building a respected club that showcases the exceptional fishing opportunities of the Mid Coast while creating lasting benefits for our members, visiting anglers and the wider community.

Our mission

Why we fish

To promote and foster the sport of game fishing through responsible angling, conservation, education and camaraderie, while providing a welcoming environment for members of all ages and experience levels.
