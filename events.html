Rules & format

How comps work

Eligible species

NSW GFA recognised species

Eligible species follow the NSW GFA and GFAA Journal rules.

Scoring

Points per the GFAA Journal

Captures and tag-and-release fish are recorded and scored in line with the GFAA Journal rules and the club's points schedule.

Weigh-in

End of each fishing day

For competitions and tournaments, weigh-in is at the end of each day's fishing. For minor points scores, arrange weigh-in with the Weighmaster.

Club competitions

Registered boats & teams

Points are awarded to CHGFC registered boats and teams. Members of other NSW GFA affiliated clubs may take part, but no points are awarded unless they are CHGFC members.

Tournaments

Prizes for visiting anglers

The same rule applies. Members of other NSW GFA affiliated clubs fishing on a CHGFC registered vessel or team (land based) may compete for prizes.

Nominations

Skippers nominate crews

Boat skippers and land-based team captains email the competition nomination form to the secretary by the Tuesday before the competition weekend.

Fish handling

Tagging, weighing & points

Results are recorded the same way for every member, in line with GFAA, NSW GFA and NSW DPIRD Fisheries requirements. For length records and identifying your catch, see the GFAA All Tackle Length Guide and Identification Guide. The NSW GFA By-Laws & Regulations (PDF) set out the association rules.

Tag & release

Tag, tackle check, record

Members hand the completed tag to a club Tag & Release / Points Scoring member, who inspects the tackle, records the points and forwards the tag to NSW GFA or NSW DPIRD Fisheries.

Capture weighing

Weighmaster checks & weighs

The Weighmaster inspects the tackle from the double to the hooks or lure, weighs compliant fish and records the result and points.

Fair results

No one marks their own catch

Club officials never assess their own tags or captures. Another authorised official does it, and unresolved results stay pending.
