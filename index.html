Chasing pelagics off the Cape.

Cape Hawke Game Fishing Club is a new, family-friendly club for offshore anglers on the Mid Coast — with club competitions, tournaments and a shared respect for the ocean.

On the water

What we're about

Competitive fishing, practical knowledge and a welcoming club culture for anglers of every generation.

Tournaments

Club comps & tournaments

Eight club competitions and four tournaments a year, with one tournament aimed at becoming a NSW GFA sanctioned major event.

Community

Learning & juniors

A welcoming club for members of all ages and experience levels, with rigging demonstration nights and free junior membership this season.

Conservation

Tag & release

Responsible angling and tag-and-release fishing, recorded and reported through the NSW GFA and NSW DPIRD Fisheries tagging programs.
