Radio calls

Say it the right way on the sked

When you read out a grid square or any letters and numbers on a sked, use the phonetic alphabet, the same as for any radio communication. Say each letter and digit on its own using the words below, so every call is clear and nobody mishears a square. Numbers use the standard radio pronunciations: 3 is "Tree", 4 is "Fower", 5 is "Fife" and 9 is "Niner".

Grid squares are called row first, then column. The row is the letter down the side of the map (with its number in front, such as 5P or 4B), and the column is the number along the top or bottom. Say every letter and digit using the words below. For example:

  • 5P 57 is called "Fife Papa, Fife Seven"
  • 4B 53 is called "Fower Bravo, Fife Tree"
  • 5X 66 is called "Fife X-ray, Six Six"
