Become a Community Partner

Every contribution is valued, whether it is cash, products, services or prizes.

Community Partner

$500 / year

Or the equivalent in products, services or prizes.

  • Recognition on the club's social media
  • Acknowledgement during club presentations
Principal Partner

$1000 and above / year

Individually tailored, or the equivalent in products, services or prizes.

  • Prominent recognition at presentations and major events
  • Sponsor banners displayed at weigh-ins and presentations
  • Naming rights to a competition, event or perpetual trophy (subject to availability)
  • Priority logo placement and dedicated social media features
Foundation Partners

Our partners

Businesses that join in the club's inaugural years will forever be recognised as Foundation Partners. Our first partners will be listed here. Partners are asked to supply high-resolution logo files, and banners where recognition includes signage.
